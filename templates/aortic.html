TECHNIQUE: The patient had a preliminary scan to obtain calcium score and was used for creating volume sets for contrasted scan. The patient then received intravenous contrast followed by a saline chase during which an overlapping sub-millimeter retrospective gated variable mAs spiral scan was obtained from a level above the aortic root through the base of the heart. Several reconstructions were performed on the source data at various points in the cardiac cycle. The reconstructed data sets were sent to an independent work station where extensive post-processing was performed that includes multiplanar 2D reformatting, 3-D volume reformatting, 3D thin slab MIP, vessel tracking, stenosis estimation, and functional evaluation. Representative images have been stored on PACS.
